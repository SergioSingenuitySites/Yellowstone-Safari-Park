# Yellowstone Safari Park light concept

Built September 29, 2026 as the second, light-mode concept for the existing `SergioSingenuitySites/Yellowstone-Safari-Park` GitHub Pages repository.

## Direction

- The drive course is the page's narrative spine. Its sticky visual is a purpose-built illustrated field map with animal markers, not a literal site map or a route drawn over a photograph.
- Warm paper, deep forest, field gold, and restrained clay accents replace the previous dark cinematic presentation.
- Motion is limited to opacity, transforms, clipping, a scroll-progress route, and the booking modal. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
- No external JavaScript, font, image, or animation libraries are used.

## Booking demo

Primary calls to action open a two-mode modal:

1. A prospect-specific Safari Park booking-flow demonstration.
2. The canonical Singenuity explainer with the approved feature cards and Sergio contact actions.

The modal is a demonstration and does not process a real reservation or payment.
